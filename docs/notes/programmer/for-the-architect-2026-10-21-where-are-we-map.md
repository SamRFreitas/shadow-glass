# For the-architect: the person can't tell where the project stands

- From: programmer, 2026-10-21, while drafting the step 2 spec.

## What happened

During Phase 1 step 2 the person said they could no longer tell where
exactly the project is: "so many phases, steps and pieces". They asked
for this to reach the-architect.

## What exists

- A complete plan does exist: the phase roadmap (0-7) and the Phase 1
  staircase (steps 1-7) in `FOUNDATION.md`, one spec per step in
  `docs/specs/`.
- `/loop-status` shows the current work, not the whole map.

## What is missing

- One place showing all levels at once with a "you are here" mark:
  project → phase → step → piece.
- Consistent words. Phase 1 uses phase → step → piece; Phase 2 was
  done as "pieces 1-13" with no steps.

## Options seen, not decided

1. A Field Notes page (`docs/*.html`) drawing the map, updated at each
   step.
2. A short "you are here" block in `FOUNDATION.md`'s roadmap.
3. `/loop-status` showing the levels above the current work.
4. Phase, step and piece defined in `docs/GLOSSARY.md`.

These can combine. To be decided with the person.

What this snapshot doesn't show

On purpose: build output (.build/, build/, ShadowGlassClient.app on the Mac), the vendored library's own huge internal tree, and anything gitignored. The point of a snapshot is the shape we authored, not what a toolchain generates or a dependency brings along.

Where this fits

The first snapshot was taken the day the Free Wings journal project was born — it is the "before" picture. This one is the "after" in a small way: the harness is now present, Phase 2 is closed, and the next milestone (Phase 1's encode piece) will be the subject of the next one of these.
